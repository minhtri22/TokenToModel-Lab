## 2. Framework selection and its limits

**Primary framework — noncompensatory hard-veto decision tree.** Rights/PII, source construct, full material fact parity, gold correctness and prereg cannot be offset by simplicity or hypothetical model performance. Apply vetoes at their phase: E0 blocks access; E1/E2/E4/E5 block actual measurement admission; E3 blocks **causal shortcut claim only**; E6 blocks model execution. No paper-only gate is falsely treated as empirical validation.

**Falsification:** every KEEP/SIMPLIFY/PIVOT/STOP/HOLD option has a discriminating future proof that would reverse its tentative choice, so unknown does not become 'impossible'.

**Qualitative FMEA:** identify high-severity failure modes and missing detection evidence; do not fabricate occurrence probability, quantitative risk-priority number, or exact cost.

**Proportionality and option value:** `CLOSED_HOLD` prevents recurring unchanged source review from posing as progress, preserves original QuestionID history and prospectively open options. `OPEN_HOLD` can be justified if there is a concrete lawful source/approval actively awaited; none appears as a **verified pending external dependency** in the frozen record, not a claim that no possible source exists.

**Why not weighted Pugh/MCDA:** No prospectively locked weights or grounded numeric source availability, sample n, power, budgets, rater effort, or option utilities. Numerical scores would produce false precision and cannot override legal/construct vetoes.
