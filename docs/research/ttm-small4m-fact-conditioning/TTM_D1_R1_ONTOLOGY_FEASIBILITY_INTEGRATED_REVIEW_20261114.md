## 4. Per-category ontology feasibility limitations

- **Entity location:** accepted A/B unique result only 1/8 under prospective physical movement; remote delivery and co-location alter shortest paths. Six-verb ontology has no independent empty-handed MOVE-agent action; do not emulate movement using irrelevant pickups.
- **Causal reasoning:** 8/8 source pairs are unique under prospective simulation, but their action-level proxy remains the same `{ACTIVATE, WAIT}` after side normalization. Existing designs switch direct trigger link against scheduled trigger without new proof of four unseen causal topologies.
- **Rule constraint:** 0/8 unique pairs under prospective physics, due to multiple shortest first actions and guard/WAIT interactions. Renaming T0/T1 or swapping side labels cannot fix the causal graph.
- **Goal persistence:** 0/8 unique under pure safety; all old tasks depend on cost-based protection-horizon tie-break or allow irrelevant successful actions. Task utility and the no-op action policy must be justified independently before claiming unique oracle.

The above limitations refer to **reviewed old 32 candidates**, not a global finite-space upper bound. An independent constructive proof or a rigorous impossibility bound remains outstanding.
